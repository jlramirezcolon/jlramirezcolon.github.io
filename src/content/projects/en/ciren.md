From 2018 to 2021, I was an Outreach Fellow with CIRE²N, the Center for Innovation, Research, and Education in Environmental Nanotechnology, an NSF-CREST research center based at the University of Puerto Rico, Río Piedras.

I worked with Caras con Causa and the Río Piedras Urban, Community, and Business Action Center on science outreach for elementary students and for the Vietnam community in Cataño, Puerto Rico.

## Along the way

- Volunteered and designed an activity on using self-assembled polymers for targeted drug delivery at the Urban, Community and Business Action Center of Capetillo (2019).
- Volunteered at the Molecular Sciences Research Center's Open House (2019).
- Designed an activity on nanotechnology and COVID-19 for the CIRE²N Spring Nanoday (2020).
- Produced a video, *Graphene: The Material of the Future*, for the CIRE²N Fall Nanoday (2020), and joined a panel of CIRE²N fellows.

## STEM for All Video Showcase 2021

I also contributed to [*STEAM Education and Outreach during COVID19 Pandemia*](https://stemforall2021.videohall.com/presentations/2263.html), CIRE²N's video for the 2021 STEM for All Video Showcase, about how the center kept its education and outreach going online during the pandemic. The video received a Public Choice Award.
