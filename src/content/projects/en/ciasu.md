From 2018 to 2020, I was president of the Círculo de Astrobiología Universitaria (CIASU), the astrobiology student group at the University of Puerto Rico, Río Piedras.

- We staffed a table at the First Puerto Rican Space Exploration Festival.
- We took part in the International Astronomical Union's IAU100 NameExoWorlds global campaign.
- We ran activities on collecting and analyzing micrometeorites.
- We visited the Arecibo Observatory.
