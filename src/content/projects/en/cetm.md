As a Science Communication Intern with Ciencia en tus Manos in the fall of 2024, I conceptualized and launched *Lunes de Ciencia Gufiá*, a Spanish-language video initiative that translates peer-reviewed research by Puerto Rican scientists into accessible scientific content.

## Making it

I produced six episodes and handled every step: literature research, story selection, scientific review, scripts, visual development, recording, editing, and publication.
