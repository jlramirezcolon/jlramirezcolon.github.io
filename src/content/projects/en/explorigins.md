ExplOrigins brings together Georgia Tech students from across disciplines who are curious about the origins of life and the exploration of space. As president from June 2025 to May 2026, I led a full year of programming.

## The year

We organized 12 events spanning science, professional development, policy, and public engagement. I coordinated speakers, participants, university partners, and community organizations, and managed logistics and programming on an annual budget of under $500. I also designed the organization's social media content and promotional materials, and facilitated discussions with early-career scientists.

Highlights from the year:

- **Hello World!: Share Your Science Stories**, an elevator pitch workshop
- **Passport to Other Worlds**, a showcase of analog sites, places on Earth that stand in for other worlds
- **Headlines from Other Cosmos**, a journal club
- **Across the Universe: A Creative Showcase**, a zine-making session
- **Horizons Beyond Earth**, a career panel with Georgia Tech alumni in astrobiology and space exploration
- **Science to Policy: Communicating Research for Impact**, a science policy workshop
- **The ExplOrigins Factor: Breaking the Ice**, the ExplOrigins Colloquium social event
- **Celebrate STEAM 2026**, at the Atlanta Science Festival
- **A field trip to Arabia Mountain**
- **Happy B-Earth Day: From Earth to What's Next**
