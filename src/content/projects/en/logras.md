From January 2022 to January 2023, I was Social and Cultural Chair of LOGRAS, the Latino Organization of Graduate Students at Georgia Tech.

I planned cultural and social activities, including Hispanic and Latinx Heritage Month trivia and community events. I also organized a discussion with Georgia Tech's Serve-Learn-Sustain on integrating the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals into graduate research.
