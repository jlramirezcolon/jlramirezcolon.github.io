For three consecutive years, I volunteered at Planeta Digital, a science fair that moves through many public spaces across Puerto Rico each year, sharing science with the public through an array of hands-on activities. Activities included the Rainbow Walking Water Experiment and a close look at phytoplankton under the microscope.

## SCIENCE LIVE!

As part of Planeta Digital, in April 2017, I presented *Contaminación Hídrica*, on water pollution, at the fair's SCIENCE LIVE! stage in Plaza Las Américas, Puerto Rico.
