As a Georgia Tech Astrobiology Fellow from May 2023 to May 2024, I coordinated interdisciplinary programming for the Astrobiology Program. That included the Fall 2023 Astrobiology Distinguished Lecture with Dr. José Carlos Aponte of NASA Goddard, a virtual special seminar on Solar System science from the James Webb Space Telescope with Dr. Naomi Rowe-Gurney, and the 2024 ExplOrigins Colloquium, *From Cosmos to Microcosm: Traversing Small Bodies to Icy Frontiers*.

I managed event communications and designed the promotional flyers, some of which appear below.
